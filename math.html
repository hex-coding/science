📖 Geometric Formulas & Theorems Cheat Sheet

1. Angles & Lines

  • Vertically Opposite Angles: Are equal ($a = b$).
  • Supplementary Angles: Sum to $180^\circ$ on a straight line.
  • Complementary Angles: Sum to $90^\circ$.
  • Parallel Lines Cut by Transversal:
    • Corresponding angles are equal ($F$-shape).
    • Alternate angles are equal ($Z$-shape).
    • Co-interior angles sum to $180^\circ$ ($C$-shape).

2. Triangles & Polygons

  • Exterior Angle Theorem: Exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
  • Interior Angle Sum of Polygon ($n$ sides): $S = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ$
  • Each Interior Angle (Regular Polygon): $\frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$
  • Exterior Angle Sum (Any Convex Polygon): $360^\circ$. Each exterior angle (regular) $= \frac{360^\circ}{n}$.
  • Euler's Formula for Convex Polyhedra: $V - E + F = 2$ ($V$ = Vertices, $E$ = Edges, $F$ = Faces).

3. Transformations

  • Reflection in Line $x=h$: $(x, y) \to (2h - x, y)$
  • Reflection in Line $y=k$: $(x, y) \to (x, 2k - y)$
  • Two Perpendicular Reflections: Equivalent to a $180^\circ$ rotation around their intersection point $(h, k)$.
  • $90^\circ$ Anticlockwise Rotation about origin: $(x, y) \to (-y, x)$

4. Congruence Tests ($\cong$)

  • SSS: Side-Side-Side
  • SAS: Side-Angle-Side (Angle must be included between the two sides).
  • AAS: Angle-Angle-Side (Two angles and a matching corresponding side).
  • RHS: Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side.
  • Ambiguous Case (SSA): Two sides and a non-included angle do not guarantee congruence (can yield 0, 1, or 2 triangles).
